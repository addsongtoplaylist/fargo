# Fargo — Brand

> **v1 — 2026-10-09. Approved by the owner.** Brand position, message order and tone of voice. Use it for the landing page, in-app copy, social posts and anything else Fargo says. Visual identity (colours, type, logo, frog) lives in `DESIGN.md`.

## Position

**Fargo is a trip planner for friends travelling together.** The plan comes first. Sharing costs is there to support the trip, never the other way round.

> **For** friends planning a trip together,
> **Fargo is** the trip planner the whole group shares,
> **so** everyone knows what's on, where to go next and that the costs are fair,
> **unlike** planners made for one person (Wanderlog, Notes) or apps that only handle money (Splitwise).

**In one line (internal):** *The trip planner for your whole group.*

**Tagline:** **Every trip starts here.** Owner decision (2026-10-09): it stays as the **hero headline** and the closing sign-off, and sits on the sign-in screen. The rest of the page (cards, features, journey) carries the "planner for the group" message.

### Fargo is a planner, not a finance app

This rule matches the guardrail in `EXPENSES.md`: "Fargo stays a travel app, not a split app."

- **Lead with the trip, every time.** Splitting costs is a supporting feature, mentioned after the planning story, never as the headline.
- **The share of attention:** at least **two thirds** of any page, post or screen talks about planning the trip and the group. Money gets **one** short mention per section at most.
- **Never look like a finance app:**
  - no wallets, charts or currency symbols as hero visuals
  - no "manage your finances"
  - no bank-app colours or language
- **Money is shown as part of a trip** (a dinner on Day 2, the hotel for the group), never as a standalone ledger.

## Message order

| Order | Pillar | What we say | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Plan it** | Day-by-day schedule, places on a map, plans that can change | Lead |
| 2 | **Plan it together** | One invite link; friends suggest ideas; everyone sees the same plan | Lead |
| 3 | **Keep it fair** | Everyone adds what they paid; Fargo works out the rest | Support, kept short |
| 4 | **Remember it** | Passport stamps, your travel stats, a trip card to share | Reward, brings people back |

**Features, not pillars:** Discover (where to eat) and Checklists support pillars 1–2. Mention them inside those stories; don't headline them.

**Personalisation** means **your trips and your stats** (Passport), not "personalised itineraries". We don't do AI recommendations, so we don't imply them.

## Tone of voice

**The organised friend in the group.** The one who made the plan, knows the hotel address and makes sure nobody's left out. Calm, warm, helpful, a little playful.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short, plain sentences | Long sentences, jargon |
| Talk about **friends, buddies, the group, the trip** | "Users", "travellers", "collaborators", "stakeholders" |
| Concrete moments: "Mei suggested Night Safari", "09:30 Gardens by the Bay" | Abstract claims: "seamless", "effortless", "all-in-one solution" |
| Light humour about group-trip chaos (300 unread messages, "what time is dinner again?") | Mocking friends, guilt-tripping, sarcasm |
| Small, true claims | Hype or claims we can't back: "AI-powered", "#1", ratings, user counts |
| Money in passing, as part of the trip | Money as the hero of the sentence |

### Words

| Prefer | Use sparingly (max once per section) | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| plan, trip, day, schedule, places, ideas, buddies, together, share, sorted, remember | costs, who paid, split, settle up, budget | finance, payment(s), transaction, wallet, ledger, debt, owe / owing, expenses manager, money app, bills, invoice |

"Who owes who" is casual and clear, but it's payment-led. Prefer "keep it fair" or "who paid what", and use "settle up" only where it's needed.

### Examples

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Plan the trip. Split the bill. One app. | Plan your trip together. |
| Know who owes who. | Everyone adds what they paid. Fargo keeps it fair. |
| Track expenses and settle debts. | Keep the trip's costs fair, without the awkward chat. |
| Personalised AI itineraries. | Your trips, your stamps, your stats. |
| Collaborate with travellers. | Plan it with your buddies. |

## What this means for the landing page

- **Hero:** keeps "Every trip starts here.".
- **Floating hero cards:** lead with planning and the group (NOW · Gardens by the Bay, Mei suggested Night Safari, Passport). The split card stays, but it's the only money item and is worded softly ("Costs shared · all square").
- **"Your trip, your way":** order Planner → Travel buddies → Budget → Passport. Rename **Budget** to **Shared costs**, with copy in the same supporting tone.
- **"One trip, start to finish":** "After the trip · Square it up" keeps both lines (owner, 2026-10-09). It's the one place money gets its own moment, at the end of the story.
- **FAQ:** keep the money questions factual and short; lead with planning questions.

## How we'll check it

- Ask 5 friend groups what made them use Fargo and how they'd describe it to a friend. If they say "it's like Splitwise", we're overselling money.
- Watch what new users do first: add an activity, invite someone, or add a cost. Planning actions should come first.
