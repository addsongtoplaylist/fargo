# Landing hero — video prompts (redesign v6)

> 2026-10-12. Six horizontal clips for the hero montage (`LANDING.md` → Redesign v6). The owner generates them and edits the montage and overlays in CapCut. Settings for all: **16:9 · 1080p · 8 s · no audio · 24 fps**. Each prompt keeps the subject in the **centre third** (so a phone crop is safe), leaves a quiet area for an overlay, and uses slow, steady motion for clean crossfades.

## 1. Japan — train window, Mount Fuji (from Singapore)

```
Cinematic 16:9 shot from inside a quiet Japanese local train, seated behind a row of warm orange leather bench seats. Through the large rounded centre window, snow-capped Mount Fuji rises above a calm, mirror-like lake on a clear morning. The train glides slowly to the left: the lake shoreline, small houses and power lines drift past outside, while Fuji stays almost still and centred because it is far away. Soft sunlight falls through the windows and slides gently across the seats. Camera locked and steady, eye level, no people. Pastel blue sky, warm interior tones, gentle film grain. Calm, peaceful travel mood. Keep Mount Fuji in the centre of the frame for the whole shot.
```
