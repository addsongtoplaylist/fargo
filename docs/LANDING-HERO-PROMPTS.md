> **v2 — 2026-10-12.** Six horizontal clips for the hero montage (`LANDING.md` → Redesign v6), generated by the owner with **Veo 3.1 text-to-video** (no start images) and edited, with the overlays, in CapCut.
> **Settings:** 16:9 · **1080p · 8 s** (Veo makes 4, 6 or 8 s clips; 1080p needs 8 s; Extend continues a clip if one ever needs to be longer) · audio off · use about 3–4 s of each in the montage.
> **Phone crop:** phones show only the middle third of a landscape clip, at full height. So every subject sits in the centre, and the overlay space is **above or below** the subject, not at the sides. Make the phone version a separate 9:16 CapCut edit and move the overlays there; don't just crop the desktop edit.
